CAD to Career · Course overview

Free CAD course overview

Learn 2D drafting and 3D product design with 53 lessons and five portfolio projects. Start with units, geometry, and drawing views, then practice constraints, parametric modeling, technical drawings, and file exchange.

What you’ll practice

  • Draft and dimension parts in the browser CAD lab and LibreCAD.
  • Build and revise parametric parts in FreeCAD or Autodesk Fusion.
  • Present your designs with drawings, models and portfolio evidence.

The 20 core lessons are part of the CAD to Career certificate curriculum. The 33 extra lessons are for practice.

Sample lessons

  • CAD units and conversions — Set up a millimeter drawing and convert real measurements.
  • Dimensioning a 2D drawing — Create a drawing another person can lay out without guessing.
  • Fully constrained CAD sketches — Control the shape, size, and position of a parametric sketch.
