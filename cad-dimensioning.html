Dimensioning a 2D drawing

Dimension the overall size of a part first, then locate each feature from consistent reference edges. Use diameter for a hole rather than its radius, and label the drawing units.

Try it

Draw a 120 × 70 mm plate with four Ø8 mm holes. Place the centers 15 mm from the nearest horizontal and vertical edges.

Check your work

From the lower-left corner the four centers are (15,15), (105,15), (15,55), and (105,55) mm. Check that someone could recreate the part using only your drawing.
