CAD units and conversions

Choose millimeters in the document before drawing. A 2-inch wide bracket measures exactly 50.8 mm because one inch equals 25.4 mm.

Try it

Sketch a 3 in × 2 in plate with one centered hole. Convert both edges to millimeters and calculate the hole center from the lower-left corner.

Check your work

The plate is 76.2 × 50.8 mm and the center is at (38.1, 25.4) mm. Verify both dimensions using your CAD tool’s measurement command.
