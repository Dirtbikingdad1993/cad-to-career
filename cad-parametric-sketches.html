Fully constrained CAD sketches

A sketch can have the right dimensions and still move. Use a reference to the origin to control its position. Use constraints for relationships such as horizontal edges and concentric circles, then add dimensions for the sizes that matter.

Try it

Sketch an 80 × 50 mm rectangle with its lower-left corner at the origin and a centered Ø12 mm circle. Change the width to 90 mm and confirm that the circle stays centered.

Check your work

At 90 mm wide the center should be at (45, 25) mm. Inspect the sketch for remaining degrees of freedom and remove redundant constraints if the solver flags them.
