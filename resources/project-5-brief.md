# CAD to Career — Project 5: Independent cable bracket

**Level:** Advanced  
**Assessment:** Human-reviewed capstone

## Objective and engineering requirements

Independently engineer an L-shaped cable bracket: base 100 × 60 × 6 mm, rear flange 100 mm wide and 6 mm thick rising 30 mm above the base. Two Ø6 mm mounting holes at (15,15) and (85,15); 30 × 10 mm through-slot centered (50,35). Document any chosen material and manufacturing considerations.

## Required evidence

Native CAD project, STEP export, dimensioned PDF, evidence of parametric editability and a 150–300 word design rationale. Reviewer approval at ≥85/100 is required.

## Skills demonstrated

- Independent problem solving
- Manufacturing drawings
- Design rationale

## Human review rubric (100 points)
- Dimensional accuracy and conformance to brief: 35 points.
- Editable native model and design intent: 25 points.
- Correct neutral exports, drawing and evidence: 20 points.
- Documentation and independent engineering rationale: 20 points.
- Pass benchmark: at least 80/100 for Projects 2–4 and 85/100 for the independent capstone.
- Approval status requires an authenticated, authorized human reviewer. No browser self-approval.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] I built and checked the geometry independently.
- [ ] Units are clear and every critical dimension is shown.
- [ ] Native source file is saved and still editable.
- [ ] Neutral exchange file and drawing are included where requested.
- [ ] I reopened exports and checked scale and orientation.
- [ ] I wrote what I designed, why, and how it can be verified.
