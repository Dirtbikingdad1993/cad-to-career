# CAD to Career — Project 1: Precision mounting plate

**Level:** Beginner  
**Assessment:** Automatic 2D geometry

## Objective and engineering requirements

Draw one 120 × 70 mm rectangle anchored at (0,0), and four holes of diameter 8 mm. Hole centers: (15,15), (105,15), (15,55), (105,55). Dimensions must be entered numerically, not estimated.

## Required evidence

Use the built-in CAD lab to save and validate your geometry. Rectangle tolerance ±0.25 mm; diameter tolerance ±0.15 mm. A passing score is 80/100 with all critical geometry correct.

## Skills demonstrated

- 2D coordinates
- Tolerance
- Hole patterns

## Automatic geometry rubric (100 points)
- Plate: one rectangle 120 × 70 mm at origin (0,0), 20 points.
- Four Ø8 mm holes, each at the specified center coordinates, 20 points each.
- Maximum plate/coordinate deviation: ±0.25 mm. Diameter deviation: ±0.15 mm.
- Exactly five supported entities; no extras. Critical geometry must all pass.
- Local checker is practice only. A backend must independently validate submitted geometry before any official credential.
