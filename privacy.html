Privacy notice · preview edition

Privacy and learning data

Student accounts and private cloud storage are now available. Practice progress may also remain cached in your browser for offline use.

Who operates CAD to Career

CAD to Career is operated by Justin Michael. Privacy questions and data requests: dirtbikingdad1993@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Guest practice is stored on the device. Signed-in users have an account with Supabase Auth and an owner-restricted cloud workspace containing lesson progress, quizzes, drawings, project notes and a display name. The same account can retrieve its data on other devices. Local caching supports offline use but unsynced changes can be lost. Export a JSON backup in Settings. Password credentials are handled by Supabase Auth and never stored in the CAD learning workspace.

Hosting and technical logs

Vercel hosts the application and may process connection metadata such as IP address, browser and request logs under its own privacy practices. No third-party advertising SDKs or analytics scripts have been added by this project.

External services

Links to Autodesk, FreeCAD, LibreCAD, Blender and Google Fonts open or fetch content from third parties, which apply their own privacy policies. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Donations

If you choose to donate, you complete payment on the payment provider's site (for example Stripe or GitHub Sponsors). That provider processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. CAD to Career does not receive or store your card number, and donating is not linked to your learning record.

Optional AI learning assistant

The Help guide works without an account and can suggest course pages without contacting an AI model. If a signed-in student uses the AI tutor when it is configured, their question, a small amount of public lesson/page context and up to four recent chat messages are sent through a protected Supabase function to OpenAI for a response. The app does not save a permanent chat transcript, and it does not automatically send private CAD files, account passwords or cloud workspace data. OpenAI may process and retain requests under its own applicable policies. AI answers may be incorrect and are not official evaluations or engineering advice.

Accounts, reviews and certificates

Supabase stores account identifiers, verification status, private project submissions, secure assessment results, reviewer decisions and certificate records. Uploaded design files are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket and are accessible only through appropriate student/reviewer authorization. Approved certificate records have a public verification endpoint exposing recipient name, program, issue date and validity. Student project files and emails are not made public by the verifier.

Data control

Use Settings to export device-local practice records and to sign out. Cloud account deletion and complete data-export requests are not yet automated. Retention terms must be finalized before wide public enrollment. To request deletion or export of cloud account data, email the operator above; do not upload sensitive or regulated plans. Supabase and Vercel process data as infrastructure providers under their published privacy terms.
