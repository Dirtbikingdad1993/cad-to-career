Privacy notice · preview edition

Privacy and learning data

This preview currently stores learning progress in your browser. Cloud accounts and private file uploads are not enabled.

Information stored on your device

Lesson confirmations, quiz scores, 2D geometry, draft project notes and an optional display name are stored in browser localStorage. They are not synchronized across devices. You can export a JSON backup or delete local data through Settings. Clearing browser storage can also erase it.

Hosting and technical logs

Vercel hosts the application and may process connection metadata such as IP address, browser and request logs under its own privacy practices. No third-party advertising SDKs or analytics scripts have been added by this project.

External services

Links to Autodesk, FreeCAD, LibreCAD, Blender and Google Fonts open or fetch content from third parties, which apply their own privacy policies. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Accounts, reviews and certificates

Cloud accounts, submissions, certificate issuance and public profiles are not live. These features must receive an updated privacy notice before activation. No local progress is automatically a verified certificate.

Data control

Use Settings to export or delete device-local records. A contact channel and legal operator details must be published before a wider public release.
