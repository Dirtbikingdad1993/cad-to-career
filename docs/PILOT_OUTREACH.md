# Training dashboard pilot — outreach and evaluation kit
Status: prospecting assets only; **0 real participating organizations confirmed**

## Who to approach first
Two community or technical education programs offering drafting, manufacturing or workforce training, and two or three small fabrication, mechanical-design or product businesses. Prioritize an instructor/manager who can approve a small test and give weekly feedback. Do not use student data before a privacy review and signed agreement.

## Discovery note (copy and customize; never send without a verified contact)
Subject: Early pilot: evidence-based CAD training dashboard

Hello,

I'm developing CAD to Career, a free CAD learning platform where students build real drafting and product-design portfolio pieces. I am looking for a small group of schools and employers to help evaluate an optional training dashboard focused on learner progress and independently reviewed project evidence.

The current dashboard is a demonstration with synthetic data, not a live student-reporting or billing service. Would you be available for a 20-minute walkthrough and feedback session? We can discuss what data would be useful, privacy requirements, and whether a limited training pilot could fit your organization.

The lessons remain free for individual learners. Any future organization service would be opt-in and separately contracted.

Thanks,
CAD to Career

## 20-minute interview script
1. What CAD or drafting training does your team currently buy or manage?
2. How do you distinguish attendance, knowledge checks, and demonstrated ability to create a deliverable?
3. Which evidence (drawing, native model, dimensions, tolerance review) would be credible to you?
4. Which reporting metric would change a purchase or training decision?
5. What learner privacy, training record retention and access requirements apply?
6. What would make a 4–6 week, 5–20 learner pilot useful?
7. Would you budget for this if it produced that outcome? Who decides?

## Pilot test cases
- Organization admin sees only its own cohort, including exports; a learner enrolled in two organizations has correct visibility for both.
- Reviewer submits an evidence decision with an audit trail; organization sees summary without private source files.
- Learner leaves a paid organization; free personal account and portfolio remain intact.
- A certificate revoked by the platform stops verifying publicly.
- Export never contains emails, raw tokens, signed storage URLs or individual performance unless separately authorized.
- 360px mobile use by instructors: account controls, stats, download and screen reader names.
