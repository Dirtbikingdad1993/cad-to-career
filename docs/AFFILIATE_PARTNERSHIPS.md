## Partnership operating rules

1. Keep **FreeCAD, Blender, LibreCAD, free exercises and no-cost learning paths** prominent even when a commercial recommendation has an affiliate option.
2. Recommend only products that genuinely match lesson or project needs. No fake reviews, no pay-to-pass requirements and no affiliate purchases as a prerequisite.
3. Disclose near every affiliate link: *“This is an affiliate link. CAD to Career may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. You do not need to purchase anything to complete the free course.”*
4. Label sponsored content separately from independent curriculum. Vendors never approve learner competencies or issue certificates.
5. Obtain privacy-compliant consent if tracking requires cookies. Never place affiliate tracking into authentication or project evidence URLs.
6. Measure actual net commission after refunds and compliance costs; do not assume it pays fixed expenses.
7. Review FTC endorsement guidance and platform/network policies before publishing links.

## Revenue and tracking review
Track applications, approvals, links used, clearly disclosed clicks, purchases (only when provided by the approved partner), refunds, net receipts and the percentage of platform revenue from affiliates. Prioritize B2B training services and other diversified sources so ads and affiliate income cannot become a dependency.
