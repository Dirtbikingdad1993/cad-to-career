Before any live paid pilot

  • Obtain organization consent, define a clear training purpose, and agree on data retention and a contract.
  • Enforce organization membership and owner-scoped row-level security on the server; never rely on a client cohort filter.
  • Invite 3–5 organizations to a time-boxed test with real learners only after privacy review and account isolation tests.
  • Charge only after the reporting delivers measurable value; billing, subscription management, and SLAs are not implemented.
  • Keep individual free learning and learner-owned portfolios completely outside the business paywall.

Verify an issued certificate · Open free lessons · Recruiting is intentionally not available.
