CAD to Career preview terms

Educational use

This website provides free technical design education, student accounts, cloud-saved practice and private project submissions. It is not a substitute for professional engineering review, workplace safety training, manufacturing verification, or legal compliance.

Credential status

Progress saved in the browser does not constitute independently verified coursework. Server-assessed quizzes, geometry checks and reviewer-gated certificates are available in the application. A credential is only issued when the server verifies all prerequisites and authorized reviewers approve advanced projects; it is not an accredited qualification. We make no guarantee of employment or professional licensure.

Design responsibility

Before manufacturing or installing any component, independently verify its dimensions, materials, safety factors, installation environment and applicable standards. Do not use unverified drawings for safety-critical components.

Software independence

Autodesk, Fusion, Blender, FreeCAD and LibreCAD are names of their respective rights holders. CAD to Career is not affiliated with or endorsed by these projects or companies. Check current software licenses before commercial use.

Preview availability

Features may change; device-local changes may be lost if disconnected from cloud sync and not exported. Do not share your password with others. Avoid sharing confidential or regulated design data. Final operator/contact details and complete release terms remain under preparation.
